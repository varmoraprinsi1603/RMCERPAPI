Ticket Escalation hota kya hai?
Maan le kisi customer ki company ka ticket aaya hai ki invoice generate nahi ho raha.

Step 1: Customer ticket raise karta hai
Ticket: TKT-013 · Priority: High
High priority ki SLA deadline 8 hours hai.




Step 2: SLA time khatam ho gaya
Support team ne ticket resolve nahi kiya aur deadline cross ho gayi.
SLA Status: SLA Breached




Step 3: Ticket Escalation
Ab ticket ko ek separate Escalated Tickets section mein dikhaya jayega, taaki Admin ya Senior Support ko pata chale ki is ticket par urgent attention chahiye.



Tere project mein kya banana hai?
Meri recommendation hai ki hum ye 3 cheezein banayein:
1. Escalated Tickets section — normal ticket list se alag.
2. Automatic identification — SLA cross ho chuki ho aur ticket abhi Resolved ya Closed na ho, toh woh escalation list mein aaye.
3. Important details — Ticket No, Company Name, Priority, Assigned To, SLA Due Date aur Status.
Example: Agar TKT-013 ki deadline nikal gayi aur ticket abhi bhi Open hai, toh woh Escalated Tickets mein dikhega. Agar ticket resolve ho gaya hai, toh usko active escalation mein nahi dikhayenge.
Ek important baat: Escalation ka matlab sirf SLA Breached ka red badge dikhana nahi hai. Is requirement mein overdue ticket ko Admin/Senior Support ke liye alag se visible banana hai. Automatic assignment ya email notification tabhi add karenge agar requirement mein chahiye.
